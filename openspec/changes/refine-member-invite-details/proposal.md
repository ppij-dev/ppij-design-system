# Refine member invitation details and bottom sheets

## Why

The invitation detail lacks the dated vertical history and email delivery state needed to understand progress. The invitation form's text scale is uneven on phones, and bottom-sheet titles sit too far below the top edge.

## Scope

Add synthetic dated invitation and email milestones to the status detail, tune invite form typography, and compact the shared bottom-sheet handle/header spacing in the admin preview.

## Acceptance

- Invitation detail has a visible vertical line, dated statuses, and email delivery state.
- Invite form labels, controls, and supporting text use a coherent scale at phone width.
- Bottom-sheet titles sit close to the compact drag handle across admin preview drawers.
